## Who We Are

AI Compare Hub is an independent review platform focused on **data-driven analysis of AI tools for developers**. No press releases, no vendor partnerships, no hype.

## Our Methodology

Every review follows our **Benchmarks-First approach**:

1. **Standardized Test Suite**: Every tool runs through 100+ automated tests (coding, reasoning, RAG accuracy)
2. **Real-World Scenarios**: We simulate production use (debugging production bugs, building APIs, migrating codebases)
3. **Cost Analysis**: We track actual API costs and performance per dollar
4. **Longevity Testing**: We don't just review v1.0 — we test stability over 2-4 weeks of daily use
